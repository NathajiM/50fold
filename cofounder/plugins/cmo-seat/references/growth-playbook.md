# Growth Playbook (template)

## Headline

**{{HEADLINE_TARGET}}.** Phases P1 and P2 are one month each; compress or stretch to your runway.

## Phase structure

### P1 — Launch-market validation (Month 1)
- Stand up the anchor channel (budget + partner count + signup target from company-context.md)
- Launch paid social: launch-market only, targeted creative test, pre-registered kill/scale rules
- Stand up basic lifecycle messaging (welcome, progress nudge, activation prompt)
- Define and instrument the funnel: signup → {{MID_FUNNEL_STEPS}} → {{ACTIVATION_EVENT}}
- **Exit criteria:** load-bearing metric at target in the launch cohort; signup → activation measurable

### P2 — Launch-market scale (Month 2)
- Double down on whichever P1 channel hit CAC payback target
- Add a micro-influencer layer (3-5 launch-market creators, paid + comped)
- Expand paid spend if CPA holds
- First PR/earned push (local first, then national trade press)
- **Exit criteria:** repeatable signup flow, CAC payback under target, anchor channel renewed or replaced

### Months 3-12 — Expansion
- Geographic/segment expansion (one new market per quarter, only if launch-market unit economics hold)
- Content/social engine (creator-led, per the delegation rules)
- Lifecycle complexity (re-engagement, lapsed-user, premium upsell)
- Brand campaign once acquisition is profitable

## Channel priorities

| Channel | Phase | Owner | Why it's here |
|---|---|---|---|
| Anchor channel | P1 | Outsourced ops | Cold-start supply |
| Paid social (primary) | P1 | Agency or AI-agent-run | Predictable, scalable |
| Lifecycle email/SMS | P1 | Outsourced tooling | Activation lift |
| Micro-influencers | P2 | Outsourced talent mgr | Trust + reach |
| PR / earned | P2 | Outsourced PR | Legitimacy |
| Secondary paid platform | P2/P3 | Outsourced creative | Demo fit, low-CAC potential |
| SEO/content | P3 | Outsourced writer | Long-tail brand search |

## What we don't do

- Anything violating the founder-time or delegation constraints
- Partnerships with competing products (hard no)
- Premature multi-market scale before launch-market unit economics prove out
