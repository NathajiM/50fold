# Paid Acquisition

The paid lead. One primary platform; others exploratory. Launch-market only until unit economics prove out.

## Steps

1. **Load context.** Read the three reference files.
2. **Identify the request type:** plan/strategy → structured budget + creative brief; audit → diagnose with funnel math; single campaign question → answer focused, no boilerplate.
3. **Always anchor to two numbers:** the CAC payback target and the load-bearing metric impact.
4. **Lead with TL;DR + recommendation,** then 2-3 options with pros/cons.

## Defaults to apply

- **Geo:** launch market only until exit criteria hit
- **Audience priority:** the primary persona first; secondary only when the load-bearing metric is healthy
- **Creative principles:** show the real outcome in the real market, not stock footage; lead with outcome, not feature
- **Test framework:** 3-creative minimum, fixed daily budget per ad set, 7-day read window
- **Kill rule:** CAC > 1.5x payback target after a pre-set impression count with statistical confidence
- **Scale rule:** CAC at or below target for 2 consecutive 7-day windows

## Channel-specific notes

**Primary platform**
- Short-form video placements first; feed second
- Automated prospecting campaigns once 50+ conversion events/week
- Persona-led creative: talent in frame, not voiceover, for persona-targeted ad sets

**Exploratory platforms**
- Creator-content ads only — never original brand creative until proven
- Hard monthly budget cap until CAC parity with primary is shown

**Search**
- Brand defense first; skip non-brand until LTV is established
- Bidding on competitor brand keywords is fine; only visible ad copy triggers trademark review

## Red-team before recommending

Before any "scale" recommendation, surface:
- Impact on the load-bearing metric?
- Does it fit the founder-time constraint?
- The kill criterion if it doesn't work?

## Hard nos

- No recommendations that violate the delegation rules (founder filming/editing/appearing).
- No expansion outside the launch market before exit criteria.
- Competitor-comparison creative is allowed where it's on-brand — but all named-competitor copy (factual AND opinion-framed) gets founder/CLO sign-off before launch, and keep reworded backups for platform-policy throttling.
