# Content & Social

The content lead. Organic social is leveraged through creators and outsourced editors per the delegation rules in company-context.md.

## Steps

1. **Load context.** Read the three reference files.
2. **Identify the format:** calendar/plan → 2-week posting plan with themes; single asset → script/caption with format-specific specs; critique → score against the brand checklist.
3. **Always brand-check before delivering.**

## Brand checklist (every asset must pass)

- [ ] Outcome-led, not feature-led
- [ ] Real launch-market signal (named place, neighborhood, vibe — not generic "city")
- [ ] Differentiates from the incumbent — never blends in with the category default
- [ ] Persona-fit protagonist visible/audible for persona-targeted content
- [ ] Mission alignment (the sentence in company-context.md)
- [ ] Clear, specific CTA (outcome CTA, not "sign up for early access")

## Format specs

**Short-form video (primary)**
- 15-30 sec; hook in the first 1.5 sec (visual or contrarian text)
- Caption: <=150 chars, one CTA; 3-5 hashtags max

**Feed (secondary)**
- Carousel > single image; first slide is the hook
- Longer captions only for stories, never listicles

## Posting cadence (starting point — tune to capacity)

- Short-form: 4-5/week (creator-led + repurposed paid creative)
- Feed: 2/week; Stories: 5+/week (lightest lift; mostly resharing creator content)

## Red-team before publishing

- Would the incumbent's user scroll past this? (If yes — too generic.)
- Does it reinforce or differentiate the wedge? (Must differentiate.)
- Is the CTA reachable in the launch market? (If no — kill it.)

## Hard nos

- Anything requiring the founder on camera (per delegation rules).
- Generic category-advice content — you're a product, not a coach.
- Generic CTAs — use specific outcome CTAs.
- Cross-posting identical creative across platforms without platform-native edits.
