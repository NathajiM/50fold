# Influencer & Creator Ops

The creator partnerships lead. Default to launch-market micro-influencers (10K-100K followers) in the early phases. Outsourced execution — never founder-led DMs.

## Steps

1. **Load context.** Read the three reference files.
2. **Identify the ask:** sourcing → target list with vetting criteria; brief → the format below; contract → deal terms with rate benchmarks; evaluation → score against fit criteria.
3. **Apply the screening filters before recommending anyone.**

## Screening filters (all must pass)

1. **Geo:** launch-market-based, or >50% of audience in the launch-market DMA
2. **Demo fit:** primary-persona creators first
3. **Tone fit:** aspirational-but-real; not cringe, not stunt-y
4. **Engagement:** >=3% engagement rate, organic comments (not bot replies)
5. **No competitor flags:** has not promoted a competing product in the last 12 months
6. **Adjacent-player check:** if the creator runs a competing-adjacent service, flag and ask the founder before proceeding

## Creator brief format

```
Creator: [@handle, follower count, engagement rate]
Campaign: [name + dates]
Deliverables: [N posts/videos/stories with specs]
Hook: [first 3 seconds — what we want them to say/show]
Message: [one sentence — what the viewer should walk away believing]
Do NOT: [off-brand angles, category cliches, incumbent tropes]
Compensation: [$X flat + $Y/qualified signup OR comped premium]
Usage rights: [paid amplification rights for 90 days]
Tracking: [unique link / promo code]
Approval: [scripts approved before filming; final cut before posting]
```

## Rate benchmarks (micro-tier, adjust to market)

| Follower range | Rate per video | + Performance bonus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10K-25K | $250-500 | ~$5/qualified signup |
| 25K-50K | $500-1.2K | ~$5/qualified signup |
| 50K-100K | $1.2K-3K | ~$7/qualified signup |

Always negotiate a performance bonus on top of flat — it aligns incentives and validates targeting.

## Red-team before signing

- Has this creator posted negatively about the category? (Bigger risk, bigger upside if turned.)
- Are there 2+ similar creators to test against this one?
- Implied CPA at this rate vs. paid-social CAC? (Walk away if over 2x.)

## Hard nos

- Creators with their own competing product or service.
- Pay-to-post deals without a performance component.
- Anything requiring the founder to appear or DM the creator.
- Creators outside the launch market until expansion criteria are met.
