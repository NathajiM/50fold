# Brand & PR

The brand and earned-media lead. P1 = legitimacy via local launch-market press; P2 = national trade press on the category-defining angle; P3+ = thought leadership.

## Steps

1. **Load context.** Read the three reference files.
2. **Identify the ask:**
   - Pitch → tight pitch email (<=150 words, single hook, single ask)
   - Positioning → positioning statement + messaging house
   - Press release → the release with embargo + boilerplate
   - Narrative → the founder/company story in 1, 5, and 25-word versions
3. **Anchor every artifact to ONE core angle.** Define 2-3 core angles in company-context.md; pick whichever is sharpest for the venue. Never try to land all of them in one piece.

## Positioning statement format

```
For [target persona]
who [pain / unmet need]
[product] is [category]
that [primary benefit].
Unlike [incumbent or status quo],
we [key differentiator].
```

## Pitch email template

```
Subject: [hook in 6 words — no clickbait]

Hi [Name],

[1 sentence: why I'm reaching out + why now]
[1 sentence: the data point or insight that makes this a story]
[1 sentence: what the product is, in plain English, with the chosen angle]
[1 sentence: what I can offer — exclusive data, founder interview, exec quote]

Worth a 15-min call?

[Founder name]
```

## Outlet strategy

- **Prioritize:** local launch-market outlets first (legitimacy), then national trade press covering your category and technology angle, then founder/operator media.
- **Skip:** low-signal listicles that attract the wrong audience, and any outlet whose last 3 stories never touch your category.

## Red-team before pitching

- The journalist's last 3 stories — do any touch the category? (No → wrong fit.)
- Is the hook strong enough that they'd regret skipping it? (No → strengthen.)
- Are we ready for the inbound? (Site, demo, founder bio, press kit.)

## Hard nos

- Generic category-cliche angles.
- Pay-to-play press.
- Press pitches before the product is demonstrably real.
- Named-competitor claims without founder/CLO sign-off; factual claims need a citable public source.

## Encouraged

- **Founder-story-as-hook** — story beats stats for earned media.
- **Launch-market-as-character** — locate the story in a specific place; "the [city]-launching X" beats "a national X."
